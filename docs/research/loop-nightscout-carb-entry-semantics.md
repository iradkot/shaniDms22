# Loop and Nightscout carb-entry semantics

Status: source-code research and product recommendation  
Evidence reviewed: 2026-08-29  
Scope: standard Loop, LoopKit, Loop's Nightscout service, and Nightscout treatment records. This note does not describe every Loop fork or every historical Nightscout version.

## Bottom line

Loop does not expose a real-world `Meal` aggregate in the reviewed carb model. It stores individual carb entries. Each new entry receives its own `syncIdentifier`; editing preserves that logical identifier and advances its `syncVersion`; deletion records a delete operation for the same entry. The reviewed entry types contain quantity, time, food type, absorption time, provenance, and synchronization metadata, but no meal, parent, or group identifier. See [`CarbStore.add/replace/delete`](https://github.com/LoopKit/LoopKit/blob/a5beee96d8e0770fc6999372a73ea5f523351a43/LoopKit/CarbKit/CarbStore.swift#L480-L647), [`CachedCarbObject` operations](https://github.com/LoopKit/LoopKit/blob/a5beee96d8e0770fc6999372a73ea5f523351a43/LoopKit/CarbKit/CachedCarbObject%2BCoreDataClass.swift#L67-L181), and [`StoredCarbEntry`](https://github.com/LoopKit/LoopKit/blob/a5beee96d8e0770fc6999372a73ea5f523351a43/LoopKit/CarbKit/StoredCarbEntry.swift#L11-L63).

For COB and glucose prediction, Loop retains those entries individually and aggregates their modeled effects. The static COB calculation adds the remaining carbs from every entry at each point in time. The dynamic model creates a separate status builder for every entry and divides observed glucose effects among entries whose absorption intervals overlap. See [`CarbMath.carbsOnBoard`](https://github.com/LoopKit/LoopKit/blob/a5beee96d8e0770fc6999372a73ea5f523351a43/LoopKit/CarbKit/CarbMath.swift#L356-L376) and [dynamic carb mapping](https://github.com/LoopKit/LoopKit/blob/a5beee96d8e0770fc6999372a73ea5f523351a43/LoopKit/CarbKit/CarbMath.swift#L794-L909).

Therefore, three additional reports of `5 g` are three distinct carb entries in Loop. Their COB and predicted glucose effects are aggregated, but Loop does not know whether they describe one continuing meal, three snacks, or an accidental duplicate. That real-world relationship is absent from the reviewed model and cannot be recovered safely from time and carb amount alone. This is an inference from the cited storage and calculation code.

## Intentional increments and accidental duplicates both exist

Official LoopDocs demonstrates an intentional incremental entry during one real-world meal: an initial `70 g` entry was followed ten minutes later by a separate `10 g` fast-carb entry. The same example later edits the original `70 g` entry to `80 g`, showing that adding another entry and editing an existing entry are both supported behaviors. See [Meal Review](https://github.com/LoopKit/loopdocs/blob/03d67df7226141637c4e22934f63c8ce84e48941/docs/operation/features/ice.md#L73-L78).

LoopDocs also discusses adding carbs while eating small amounts over time and separately warns users to remove accidental double meal entries. It does not provide an automatic rule that distinguishes an intentional increment from a duplicate. See [Check COB Before Adding More and Avoid Double Meal Entries](https://github.com/LoopKit/loopdocs/blob/03d67df7226141637c4e22934f63c8ce84e48941/docs/operation/features/carbs.md#L223-L237).

The safe terminology is therefore **Loop Carb Entry** or **Nightscout Carb Treatment**, not a claim that each record is a separate real-world meal.

## What Loop uploads to Nightscout

Loop's Nightscout service maps one `SyncCarbObject` to one Nightscout `Carb Correction` treatment. The upload includes the carb-entry start time, rounded grams, absorption time, food type, Loop `syncIdentifier`, user-created and user-modified timestamps, and an `enteredBy` value beginning with `loop://`. See [`SyncCarbObject.carbCorrectionNightscoutTreatment`](https://github.com/LoopKit/NightscoutService/blob/4f89c9a79794110919b4ad8f8094c621ebc4135b/NightscoutServiceKit/Extensions/SyncCarbObject.swift#L14-L30) and [`CarbCorrectionNightscoutTreatment`](https://github.com/LoopKit/NightscoutKit/blob/4ec9fd12a16b5d6c2de4f11870511361d42e1b7f/Sources/NightscoutKit/Models/Treatments/CarbCorrectionNightscoutTreatment.swift#L11-L106).

Loop uploads insulin doses through a separate path. A bolus becomes a `Correction Bolus` treatment rather than being embedded in the carb treatment. See [`DoseEntry.treatment`](https://github.com/LoopKit/NightscoutService/blob/4f89c9a79794110919b4ad8f8094c621ebc4135b/NightscoutServiceKit/Extensions/DoseEntry.swift#L12-L65) and [`BolusNightscoutTreatment`](https://github.com/LoopKit/NightscoutKit/blob/4ec9fd12a16b5d6c2de4f11870511361d42e1b7f/Sources/NightscoutKit/Models/Treatments/BolusNightscoutTreatment.swift#L24-L64). A carb record and a nearby bolus record therefore should not be assumed to share one meal identity merely because their timestamps are close.

The Nightscout treatment schema supports fields such as `_id`, `eventType`, `created_at`, `enteredBy`, `notes`, `carbs`, `foodType`, and `absorptionTime`. Its synchronization fields include `identifier`, Loop's `syncIdentifier`, xDrip's `uuid`, and pump-specific identifiers. The reviewed schema defines no universal meal, group, or parent-treatment identifier. See [Nightscout treatment core and nutrition fields](https://github.com/nightscout/cgm-remote-monitor/blob/7e0e77f88fc113a76fe363504125f5b36b8a3fe3/docs/data-schemas/treatments-schema.md#L216-L246) and [sync/reconciliation fields](https://github.com/nightscout/cgm-remote-monitor/blob/7e0e77f88fc113a76fe363504125f5b36b8a3fe3/docs/data-schemas/treatments-schema.md#L295-L328).

Nightscout documents `enteredBy` as free-form and not identity-verified. It is useful as a display hint, not as proof that a trusted Loop instance created the record. See [`enteredBy` behavior](https://github.com/nightscout/cgm-remote-monitor/blob/7e0e77f88fc113a76fe363504125f5b36b8a3fe3/docs/data-schemas/treatments-schema.md#L399-L405).

## Identity, editing, and deletion

| Layer                    | Relevant identity                                                                                                                                                                        | Edit and delete behavior                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Loop carb store          | `syncIdentifier` is the logical synchronization identity; `syncVersion` advances on edit. A HealthKit `uuid`, provenance, and user-created/updated/deleted timestamps are also retained. | Editing preserves the `syncIdentifier`; deletion creates a delete operation for later synchronization. [`CachedCarbObject` operations](https://github.com/LoopKit/LoopKit/blob/a5beee96d8e0770fc6999372a73ea5f523351a43/LoopKit/CarbKit/CachedCarbObject%2BCoreDataClass.swift#L67-L181)                           |
| Loop Nightscout uploader | Loop uploads the `syncIdentifier`, then stores a mapping from it to Nightscout's object ID.                                                                                              | Updates resolve `syncIdentifier` to the cached Nightscout object ID; deletes send Nightscout object IDs. [`NightscoutUploader`](https://github.com/LoopKit/NightscoutService/blob/4f89c9a79794110919b4ad8f8094c621ebc4135b/NightscoutServiceKit/Extensions/NightscoutUploader.swift#L14-L73)                       |
| Nightscout API v1        | `_id` is the server-assigned MongoDB identity.                                                                                                                                           | `PUT /treatments/` saves an updated record; `DELETE /treatments/:id` deletes by `_id`. [v1 treatment routes](https://github.com/nightscout/cgm-remote-monitor/blob/7e0e77f88fc113a76fe363504125f5b36b8a3fe3/lib/api/treatments/index.js#L147-L205)                                                                 |
| Nightscout API v3        | `identifier` is the API-facing document identity; `_id` remains internal.                                                                                                                | `PUT`, `PATCH`, and `DELETE` address `/{collection}/{identifier}`. Deletion is normally represented as deleted history unless permanent deletion is requested. [API v3 operations](https://github.com/nightscout/cgm-remote-monitor/blob/7e0e77f88fc113a76fe363504125f5b36b8a3fe3/lib/api3/swagger.yaml#L180-L378) |

In Nightscout v15.0.7, Loop carb and dose `syncIdentifier` values are preserved but are not copied into API v3's `identifier`; Loop's uploader continues to rely on its local object-ID cache. See [Nightscout identifier normalization](https://github.com/nightscout/cgm-remote-monitor/blob/7e0e77f88fc113a76fe363504125f5b36b8a3fe3/docs/data-schemas/treatments-schema.md#L295-L328).

## Read direction in standard Loop

Current LoopDocs says standard Loop does not read arbitrary carb entries from Nightscout or a Medtronic pump; users must use Loop's meal-entry tool. See [Loop 3 display documentation](https://github.com/LoopKit/loopdocs/blob/03d67df7226141637c4e22934f63c8ce84e48941/docs/loop-3/displays-v3.md#L188-L202).

Loop also offers a separate remote-command workflow in which a caregiver can send a carb command through Nightscout to the Looper's phone. That command path is distinct from treating every Nightscout carb treatment as Loop input. See [Loop remote-control FAQ](https://github.com/LoopKit/loopdocs/blob/03d67df7226141637c4e22934f63c8ce84e48941/docs/faqs/remote-control.md#L1-L24).

For the standard configuration reviewed here, it is reasonable to treat Loop-originated Nightscout carb treatments as an uploaded view of Loop entries, while keeping ShaniDms's V1 integration read-only. This is a product inference from the documented upload and read paths, not a guarantee for forks or custom remote-command configurations.

## Product implications for ShaniDms

1. A local Meal Entry may link to zero, one, or many Nightscout carb treatments, but each link must be explicitly confirmed by the Product User. Time and grams may rank candidates only; they cannot establish meal membership because intentional incremental entries and accidental duplicates are both documented behaviors. [Evidence](https://github.com/LoopKit/loopdocs/blob/03d67df7226141637c4e22934f63c8ce84e48941/docs/operation/features/carbs.md#L223-L237)
2. Preserve every linked external record independently, including its external identifiers, event time, grams, absorption time, food type, and source metadata. Do not rewrite several treatments into one synthetic Nightscout record because Loop models each entry separately. [Evidence](https://github.com/LoopKit/LoopKit/blob/a5beee96d8e0770fc6999372a73ea5f523351a43/LoopKit/CarbKit/CarbMath.swift#L356-L376)
3. The UI may show `Food logged: 30 g` and `Reported to Loop: 15 g + 5 g + 5 g`, with a derived reported total, while keeping all components inspectable. The total is presentation data, not a new external identity. This follows from Loop's per-entry storage and aggregate calculation. [Evidence](https://github.com/LoopKit/LoopKit/blob/a5beee96d8e0770fc6999372a73ea5f523351a43/LoopKit/CarbKit/CarbMath.swift#L356-L376)
4. Define a duplicate as the same external record identity received more than once, not as equal carbs within a time window. Persist the Nightscout Workspace/source together with every available `_id`, `identifier`, and `syncIdentifier`, because their meaning and availability differ by API and client. [Evidence](https://github.com/nightscout/cgm-remote-monitor/blob/7e0e77f88fc113a76fe363504125f5b36b8a3fe3/docs/data-schemas/treatments-schema.md#L295-L328)
5. Treat a nearby bolus as contextual data unless the Product User explicitly associates it. Loop uploads carb and dose treatments separately and provides no shared meal identity between them. [Evidence](https://github.com/LoopKit/NightscoutService/blob/4f89c9a79794110919b4ad8f8094c621ebc4135b/NightscoutServiceKit/Extensions/NightscoutUploader.swift#L14-L73)
6. Never use `enteredBy` alone for deduplication, authorization, or trusted source attribution because Nightscout defines it as an unverified free-form value. [Evidence](https://github.com/nightscout/cgm-remote-monitor/blob/7e0e77f88fc113a76fe363504125f5b36b8a3fe3/docs/data-schemas/treatments-schema.md#L399-L405)

## Version and compatibility caveats

- The LoopKit calculation and storage findings are pinned to [`LoopKit/LoopKit` commit `a5beee9`](https://github.com/LoopKit/LoopKit/tree/a5beee96d8e0770fc6999372a73ea5f523351a43), and the Nightscout upload mapping is pinned to [`LoopKit/NightscoutService` commit `4f89c9a`](https://github.com/LoopKit/NightscoutService/tree/4f89c9a79794110919b4ad8f8094c621ebc4135b). They were the respective `dev` heads reviewed on 2026-08-29.
- The Nightscout findings are pinned to [`cgm-remote-monitor` commit `7e0e77f`](https://github.com/nightscout/cgm-remote-monitor/tree/7e0e77f88fc113a76fe363504125f5b36b8a3fe3), corresponding to v15.0.7 source. Older installations may lack its UUID/`identifier` normalization behavior. [Version-specific behavior](https://github.com/nightscout/cgm-remote-monitor/blob/7e0e77f88fc113a76fe363504125f5b36b8a3fe3/docs/data-schemas/treatments-schema.md#L306-L328)
- Nightscout's `eventType` is effectively free-form, and controller-specific clients may add fields and types. Trio, iAPS, AAPS, older Loop releases, and custom builds must be feature-detected from their actual records rather than assumed to match standard Loop. [Nightscout event-type caveat](https://github.com/nightscout/cgm-remote-monitor/blob/7e0e77f88fc113a76fe363504125f5b36b8a3fe3/docs/data-schemas/treatments-schema.md#L331-L376)
- Loop's remote-carb commands are experimental/configuration-dependent and should not be conflated with ordinary Nightscout treatment ingestion. [Loop remote-control documentation](https://github.com/LoopKit/loopdocs/blob/03d67df7226141637c4e22934f63c8ce84e48941/docs/faqs/remote-control.md#L1-L24)
